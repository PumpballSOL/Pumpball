# CODE OF CONDUCT

Be direct. Be kind. Keep it clean.

## Do

- Give specific, constructive feedback
- Assume good faith until someone shows otherwise
- Call out scams and impersonators loudly

## Don't

- Harass, threaten or dox anyone
- Shill other tokens or post referral links in issues and PRs
- Post seed phrases, private keys or wallet drains. Ever
- Pressure anyone to buy, or promise returns

Maintainers may edit, close or delete anything that breaks these rules, and may block repeat offenders.

Report problems by opening an issue or through the contact in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).
