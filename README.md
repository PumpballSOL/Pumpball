**PUMPBALL is a Powerball-style token built directly on Pump.fun.** No custom launchpad, no separate platform. Pump.fun token activity generates fees, those fees accumulate into a prize pool, and at predetermined drawings eligible PUMPBALL holders have a chance to win it.

**Pump.fun + Powerball.** A crypto-native lottery machine, running on top of Pump.fun.

<br />

---

## `[ 01 ]` HOW IT WORKS

```text
   PUMP.FUN
      │
      ▼
 TRADING FEES
      │
      ▼
   PUMPBALL
      │
      ▼
  PRIZE POOL
      │
      ▼
     DRAW
      │
      ▼
    WINNER
```

| Stage | What happens |
| :--- | :--- |
| **PUMP.FUN** | PUMPBALL lives natively on Pump.fun. Nothing custom to launch or trust. |
| **TRADING FEES** | Trading activity on the token generates fees. |
| **PUMPBALL** | Fees are routed to the PUMPBALL prize pool. |
| **PRIZE POOL** | The pool accumulates between draws. |
| **DRAW** | At a predetermined drawing, eligible holders are entered. |
| **WINNER** | One wallet wins the accumulated prize. |

## `[ 07 ]` FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>What is PUMPBALL?</b></summary>

<br />

A token on Pump.fun where trading fees accumulate into a prize pool that is drawn to a holder at predetermined drawings.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is there a custom launchpad?</b></summary>

<br />

No. PUMPBALL is built directly on Pump.fun.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I enter?</b></summary>

<br />

Buy PUMPBALL and hold it. Eligibility rules will be published in the transparency section.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How much can I win?</b></summary>

<br />

The prize is whatever has accumulated in the pool. Nothing is guaranteed, and pool size depends entirely on trading activity.

</details>
