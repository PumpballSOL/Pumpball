# FAQ

### What is PUMPBALL?
A Powerball-style token built directly on Pump.fun. Token activity generates fees, the fees accumulate into a prize pool, and at predetermined drawings eligible holders have a chance to win it.

### Is there a custom launchpad?
No. PUMPBALL lives on Pump.fun.

### How do I enter?
Buy PUMPBALL and hold it. The exact eligibility rules are `TBA` and will be published in [DRAW_RULES.md](./DRAW_RULES.md).

### Where does the prize come from?
From fees generated by Pump.fun token activity, accumulated into the prize pool.

### How much can I win?
Whatever has accumulated in the pool at the draw. Nothing is guaranteed and the size depends on trading activity.
